Getting Started with Painting

I believe anyone can paint and if you enjoy it you should go for it and not be afraid of if it turns out "good" or not. While learning technique is great and might be something you pursue later on if you enjoy painting, all you need to start is the painting medium you desire to use and a surface to paint on.

What Supplies You Need

To get started with painting you will need supplies! Some paint and pigment such as watercolor, gouache, and pastel often work best on paper, while others like oil and acrylic usually work best on canvas. Different types of paint work best with different kinds of paintbrushes. Finally, some paint can be layered on top of each other such as gouache on watercolor and oil on acrylic, the reverse isn't true such as putting acrylic on oil or watercolor on gouache because of the composition of the paint. How to Decide What to Paint
Now that you have your supplies, all that is left is to decide what to paint! In my opinion, deciding what to paint is often the hardest part of painting. I'm learning that it's better not to overthink it too much and just paint what I'm passionate about at the moment. Sometimes I paint abstract paintings and other times I reference my favorite photos I've taken of wildlife on nature walks. Another great way to find new ideas is to gather inspiration from drawing prompts or create a moodboard with the general tone you want your painting to have. Inspiration is everywhere; you just have to be looking for it!
