Painting

One of my favorite hobbies is painting. There are so many possibilities of what to paint and what materials to use when painting.

My Favorite Painting Mediums

  • Watercolor
  • Acrylic
  • Gouache
  • Oil
  • Soft Pastel

How I Decide What To Paint

In my opinion, deciding what to paint is often the hardest part of painting. I'm learning that it's better not to overthink it too much and just paint what I'm passionate about at the moment. Sometimes I paint abstract paintings and other times I reference my favorite photos I've taken of wildflife on nature walks.

Do You Want To Paint?

I believe anyone can paint and if you enjoy it you should go for it and not be afaid of if it turns out "good" or not. While learning technique is great and might be something you pursue later on if you enjoy painting, all you need to start is the painting medium you desire to use and a surface to paint on.
